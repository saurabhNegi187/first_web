About Vidyamrit

A Coaching institute in Roorkee led by IITians and experienced teachers with a simple belief — that focused, ethical teaching and genuine care can change where a child’s life goes next.

Roorkee, Uttarakhand Class 6–10
Who We Are

Guiding young learners since 2026

Vidyamrit began as a single classroom with a handful of students preparing for the Navodaya entrance. Ten years on, we prepare hundreds of Class 6–10 students each year for Navodaya, Army & Sainik School, Foundation and Board examinations — without ever losing the personal, family-like attention we started with.
