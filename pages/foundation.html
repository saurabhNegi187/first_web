Foundation Course (Class 6–8)

A strong academic base for Classes 6, 7 and 8 — mastering Maths, Science and English while building the study habits that carry a child for years.

Class 6–8 School + Beyond Olympiad Ready Concept-First

Programme Overview

The middle-school years decide how confident a student feels in Maths and Science later on. This is where habits — good or bad — quietly take root.

Our Foundation programme stays aligned with the school syllabus (NCERT / CBSE / State) while going a step deeper, so children truly understand rather than memorise. We layer in gentle exposure to Olympiads and NTSE-style thinking to stretch curious minds.

Above all, we teach students how to study — how to make notes, plan revision and approach a problem calmly.

Key Features

  • NCERT-aligned teaching for Class 6, 7 & 8
  • Conceptual Maths & Science with real examples
  • English reading, writing & grammar practice
  • Olympiad & NTSE-style enrichment
  • Regular tests with study-habit coaching
  • Activity-based, engaging classroom style

FAQs

Foundation course — your questions answered

Does the course follow my child’s school syllabus?
Yes. We stay aligned with the NCERT/CBSE/State syllabus so it supports school directly, then add a little depth for stronger understanding.
Can my child join for just one subject?
Absolutely. Single-subject enrolment is available for Maths, Science or English if you want focused help in one area.
Do you help with Olympiads and NTSE?
Our optional enrichment track introduces Olympiad and NTSE-style questions to build higher-order thinking without overwhelming the student.
Is there a trial class before joining?
Yes, we encourage a free trial class so your child can experience our teaching style before enrolling.
