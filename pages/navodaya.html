Programme Overview

The Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) is one of India’s most competitive entrance exams for Class 6 admission — and one of the most rewarding.

Our Navodaya programme is built around the exact JNVST blueprint: mental ability, arithmetic and language. We combine daily concept classes, pattern-based worksheets and full-length mock tests so that young students walk into the exam hall calm, quick and confident.

Because JNVST rewards reasoning as much as knowledge, we spend real time on non-verbal puzzles, figure series and spatial thinking — the areas where most first-timers lose marks.

FAQs

Navodaya coaching — your questions answered

When should my child start JNVST preparation?
Ideally at the start of Class 5. A full 10–12 month runway lets us build concepts calmly and fit in enough mock tests. We also run a shorter crash course for late starters.
Is the coaching available in Hindi medium?
Yes. Teaching and material are bilingual, and the language section is practised in the medium your child will choose for the exam.
How many students are there per batch?
Batches are deliberately kept small so every child gets individual attention and their doubts are actually heard and resolved.
Do you provide previous years’ question papers?
Absolutely. Solved previous-year papers and a full mock-test series in the real JNVST format are part of the course material.
