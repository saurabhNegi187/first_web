Privacy Policy

How Vidyamrit Coaching Institute collects, uses and protects the information you share with us.

Last updated: January 2025 · This is a sample policy provided for demonstration and should be reviewed by a qualified professional before publication.

1. Introduction

Vidyamrit Coaching Institute (“Vidyamrit”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and enquiry forms, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or submitting an enquiry, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an admission enquiry or contact form, we may collect:

  • The student’s name and current class or school
  • Parent or guardian name
  • Contact details such as mobile number, WhatsApp number and email address
  • Your city and the programme you are interested in
  • Any message or additional information you choose to share

We may also collect limited, non-identifying technical information (such as browser type or pages visited) if analytics tools are enabled in future.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and arrange counselling or a diagnostic test
  • Recommend a suitable programme and share batch, fee and schedule details
  • Communicate with you about admissions and, if you consent, about workshops, guest lectures and updates
  • Maintain our own internal records and improve our services

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. Information may be handled by our own staff and trusted service providers (for example, a hosting or messaging provider) strictly to operate the services described above, or where required by law.

5. Data Retention

We keep enquiry information only for as long as necessary to respond to you and for reasonable record-keeping. You may ask us to delete your details at any time, subject to any legal obligations we may have.

6. Children’s Privacy

Because our services relate to school-age children, enquiries are expected to be made by a parent or guardian. We ask that students do not submit their own personal details without a parent’s involvement, and we handle any information about a child with particular care.

7. Cookies & Analytics

This website currently uses only what is necessary to function. If cookies or analytics are introduced in future, this policy will be updated to explain what is used and how you can manage your preferences.

8. Your Choices & Rights

You may contact us at any time to review, correct or delete the information you have shared, or to withdraw consent for optional communications. We will respond to reasonable requests promptly.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out:

  • By email at admissions@vidyamrit.edu.in
  • By phone at +91 98290 00000
  • In person at 12, Shiksha Marg, Vidyadhar Nagar, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302039

You can also use our contact page to send us a message.
