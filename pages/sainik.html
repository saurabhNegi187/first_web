Army & Sainik School Entrance Preparation

Structured preparation for the All India Sainik School Entrance Exam (AISSEE) and Military School admissions — where discipline, aptitude and academics come together.

Class 6 & 9 Entry AISSEE Pattern Maths · GK · Reasoning Interview & Medical Guidance

Programme Overview

A seat in a Sainik or Military School is the first step towards the National Defence Academy and a life in uniform — and it starts with a demanding written exam.

We prepare students for AISSEE at both entry points: Class 6 (Mathematics, Language, Intelligence and General Knowledge) and Class 9 (Mathematics, English, Science and Social Science). Alongside the written test, we guide families through the medical standards and interview stage so nothing comes as a surprise.

Because these exams use OMR sheets and reward speed under pressure, we build exam temperament early with timed, full-length practice.

FAQs

Army & Sainik prep — your questions answered

Which classes can apply for Sainik Schools?
Admission is mainly at Class 6 and Class 9 through AISSEE. We run separate batches for each entry point with the correct subjects and difficulty.
Do you prepare students for the interview and medical too?
Yes. Beyond the written test we brief families on the medical standards and run mock interviews so students present themselves with confidence.
Is coaching available in Hindi medium?
Teaching and material are bilingual, and students practise in the medium they will opt for in the exam.
How is the intelligence section handled?
It gets dedicated daily practice — verbal and non-verbal reasoning, patterns and puzzles — since it is where many first-timers lose easy marks.
